## 0. Executive Summary

### Top 3 Missing Signals (across all horizons)
1. **Gamma exposure / zero-gamma level (0DTE pin & trend regime).** The MCP server exposes `gamma_exposure_profile` (returns net GEX, zero-gamma level, dealer regime) but **no agent uses it**. This is the single highest-leverage 0DTE signal we own and ignore.
2. **IV term structure shape (event-driven dislocation).** `iv_term_structure` returns BACKWARDATION / KINKED / CONTANGO and the kink expiry. Critical for both 0DTE (front-end panic) and earnings (kink at the event). Currently unused.
3. **Position rolling (thesis durability for swing/LEAP).** `position_rolling_detector` flags institutions rolling near-term contracts forward — a high-signal "conviction is intact, just being termed out" indicator. Currently unused.

### Top 3 Unused Tools
1. `gamma_exposure_profile` (options_flow.py) — dealer GEX & zero-gamma level
2. `iv_term_structure` (options_flow.py) — front/back IV shape, KINKED/BACKWARDATION detection
3. `position_rolling_detector` (oi_changes.py) — institutional roll detection
   *(Honorable mentions: `expiry_heatmap`, `greek_screener`, `multileg_activity`, `signal_backtest`, `trend_analyzer`, `oi_trend`, `conviction_matrix`, `daily_synthesis`, `largest_dark_pool_trades`, `extended_hours_filter`, `dark_pool_price_levels`, `top_premium_trades`.)*

### Top 2 New Agents to Build First
1. **`gamma-flip-tracker`** — 0DTE pin/anti-pin radar. Reads `gamma_exposure_profile`, `expiry_heatmap`, `iv_term_structure`. Tells you whether SPX/SPY/QQQ and any liquid name will pin or trend into the close.
2. **`leap-positioning-radar`** — Long-dated whale OI builds. Reads `oi_trend` (multi-day), `biggest_oi_increases` (filtered DTE > 180), `position_rolling_detector`, `largest_dark_pool_trades`, `conviction_matrix`. Currently nothing in the system covers the 6-24mo horizon.

### Top 1 Orchestration Change
**Split the daily report by horizon (0DTE / Swing / LEAP) instead of by agent.** A trader doesn't read by "what sweep-tracker found" — they read by "what's my 0DTE play, what's my swing book, what LEAPs am I building." The current structure (Sweeps section, Accumulation section, etc.) silos the agents but loses the trader's mental model. Restructure: `Regime → 0DTE Block → Swing Block → LEAP Block → Cross-horizon convictions → Risk`.

## 1. Trader Edge Framework — Data → Signal → Trigger → Invalidation

Derived independently of the current agents. Each horizon has 7–10 must-have signals.

### 1.A — 0DTE / Intraday (hours)

The 0DTE edge is **dealer hedging flow + gamma regime**. Direction matters less than knowing if the tape will pin or trend.

| # | Signal | Why it matters | Trigger | Invalidation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0D-1 | **Net dealer GEX & zero-gamma level** | Positive GEX → pinning, sell premium / fade extremes. Negative GEX → trending, buy gamma / chase. | Spot crosses zero-gamma in either direction with > X% velocity | Spot reverts back through zero-gamma; GEX flips sign mid-session |
| 0D-2 | **0DTE ATM gamma flip strike** | Defines the magnet (high-OI ATM call/put walls) and breakout strike | Spot pierces nearest gamma wall on size | Spot fails to hold breakout for > 15 min after pierce |
| 0D-3 | **Front-end IV (VIX1D / 0DTE IV)** | Backwardation in front-end IV = panic / event imminent | VIX1D > 1m IV by > 1 stdev | Front IV collapses back into contango |
| 0D-4 | **Real-time sweep velocity (last 30/60 min)** | Acceleration of aggressive sweeps tells you who's chasing | Cumulative ask-side premium > 2x rolling avg | Sweep tape goes mixed (calls + puts) or reverses |
| 0D-5 | **Charm/vanna into close** | Last 90 min: dealers re-hedge as theta accelerates → systematic flows | Position relative to large 0DTE OI walls and IV change | Major macro headline overrides flow |
| 0D-6 | **VWAP / spot vs nearest high-OI strike** | Max-pain magnet effect into close | Spot pinned within 0.3% of strike with declining range | Volume expansion through strike |
| 0D-7 | **Today's expiry volume vs total** | High 0DTE share = retail-driven; low = institutional | 0DTE share > 40% of total option volume | — (descriptive) |
| 0D-8 | **Cross-asset divergence (ES/NQ vs SPY)** | NQ leading = tech-led, breadth divergence flags fragility | Sustained beta divergence > 30 min | Convergence resumes |

### 1.B — Swing (1–6 weeks)

The swing edge is **multi-session conviction confirmed across multiple data layers**. You're looking for institutional fingerprints accreting over days.

| # | Signal | Why it matters | Trigger | Invalidation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SW-1 | **Multi-day OI buildup, consistent direction** | New money sticking, not flipping | 3+ consecutive sessions of net OI build, same option type | Single-day OI reversal + price break |
| SW-2 | **Dark-pool late-tape % of ADV** | Institutional accumulation/distribution that doesn't move tape | DP volume > 25% of ADV across 3+ sessions, buy-skewed | Buy/sell ratio flips; price breaks below DP price levels |
| SW-3 | **Repeated sweeps over multiple sessions** | One-day sweep is news; multi-day sweep is a campaign | Same ticker shows top-10 sweep volume on 2+ sessions in a week | Sweeps stop or flip direction |
| SW-4 | **IV term-structure shape (kink at expiry)** | Kink = market pricing a discrete event at that expiry | KINKED structure, kink expiry within 30 days | Kink dissipates or moves further out |
| SW-5 | **P/C premium ratio z-score** | Extremes mark sentiment edges | > 2 stdev from 20-day mean | Mean revert to within 1 stdev |
| SW-6 | **Sector flow rotation (3-5 day persistence)** | Sector cycles bleed into single-name trades | Sector net flow flips and persists 3+ days | Sector flow reverts |
| SW-7 | **Position rolling forward** | Institutions rolling = "thesis intact, just buying time" | Roll detected with balance ratio > 0.7 | Net OI decrease without offsetting far-dated build |
| SW-8 | **Smart money flow (ask vs bid premium)** | Confirms direction beyond raw volume | Net ask-side premium > 1.5x bid for 2+ sessions | Flips bid-skewed |
| SW-9 | **Analyst vs flow disagreement** | When pros disagree with analysts, pros usually win | Analyst bullish, flow bearish (or vice versa) | Both align |
| SW-10 | **Multileg/spread positioning** | Spreads imply specific directional thesis (calendar, fly, vertical) | Multileg ratio > 30% with consistent structure | Single-leg flow dominates |

### 1.C — LEAPs (6–24 months)

The LEAP edge is **structural, low-frequency, conviction-weighted**. You're co-investing alongside long-only institutions, not trading flow.

| # | Signal | Why it matters | Trigger | Invalidation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LP-1 | **Long-dated whale OI (DTE > 180) builds** | Institutional thesis with multi-quarter horizon | Single ticker shows top-N OI growth at LEAP expiries over 30d | OI rolls back near-term or unwinds |
| LP-2 | **Conviction matrix scenario** | DIRECTIONAL_LONG vs HEDGED_LONG vs COVERED_CALL has very different implications | Persistent DIRECTIONAL_LONG over 60+ days | Scenario flips to HEDGED_LONG or MIXED |
| LP-3 | **Cumulative net premium flow (60/90d)** | Slow accretion is the LEAP signature | Top decile net cumulative flow over rolling 90d window | Flow reverses for > 2 weeks |
| LP-4 | **Recurring dark-pool price levels** | Institutional defense / accumulation zones | DP volume cluster > 5% of float at one level over 30d | Price breaks decisively below the level |
| LP-5 | **Macro regime alignment** | LEAPs only work in regimes that survive 6+ months | Bullish-trending market regime + sector tailwind | Regime flips to bearish-trending |
| LP-6 | **Long-dated skew (back-month put skew)** | Back-month puts cheap = market not pricing tail | Back-month skew at 1y low | Skew steepens > 1 stdev |
| LP-7 | **Realized vs implied vs historical vol (long windows)** | LEAP IV mispricing | Long-dated IV in bottom decile of 1y range | IV expands > 1 stdev |
| LP-8 | **Insider / fundamental confirmation** | Yahoo fundamentals (PE, marketcap, short %, recommendations) align | Bullish flow + improving fundamentals + analyst upgrade trend | Fundamentals deteriorate |
| LP-9 | **Sector persistence (60d+)** | Sector tailwind must outlast the trade | Sector net flow positive in 60% of last 60 sessions | Sector breadth deteriorates |

### 2.C — What succeeds vs what fails (with quoted lines)

**Successes:**
- `accumulation-hunter.md:14` — `"Flag tickers where 3+ signals align."` Strong false-positive control via signal stacking.
- `contrarian-scanner.md:18` — `"**Risk** — what would invalidate the fade"` Only agent that explicitly demands invalidation in the output schema.
- `contrarian-scanner.md:12` — `"only fade in range-bound or high-vol regimes; avoid fading in strong trending markets"` Good regime-conditional logic.
- `sweep-tracker.md:14-18` — Filter rules (consistent direction, near-term expiry, large premium, elevated underlying volume) are concrete and trader-grade.

**Failures:**
- **No agent uses gamma data.** None of the 5 agent files contain the strings `gamma`, `gex`, or `zero_gamma`. The single highest-leverage 0DTE tool (`gamma_exposure_profile`) is invisible to the system.
- **No agent uses term structure.** None reference `iv_term_structure` despite it returning the directly-tradeable `KINKED`/`BACKWARDATION` enum.
- **No multi-day analysis.** Despite `oi_trend`, `trend_analyzer`, and `signal_backtest` being available in `historical.py`, no agent reads multi-session history. Every signal in every agent is single-day.
- `sweep-tracker.md:11` — `"most_active_contracts — which specific strikes and expiries are being targeted?"` Good intent, but never crosses with `gamma_exposure_profile` to ask whether those strikes are dealer-short-gamma (high-impact) or dealer-long-gamma (absorbed).
- `risk-monitor.md:9` — uses `portfolio_correlation` but the agent never *takes* a watchlist input from earlier agents — so it correlates "the watchlist" not "today's high-conviction setups," weakening the loop.
- `earnings-scout.md:10` — uses IV rank (which is 30-day IV percentile) as the IV regime measure, missing `iv_term_structure` which would tell you whether the kink sits *at* the earnings expiry (the actual edge).
- **No agent covers LEAPs at all.** Every agent prompt is implicitly short-dated. `biggest_oi_increases` calls in agents are never DTE-filtered to long-dated.
- **`/daily-analysis` orchestration** (`daily-analysis.md:24-54`) is structured by *agent output*, not by *trade horizon*. A trader scanning for "what's my swing book today" has to read across all 5 sections and re-cluster manually.
- `daily-analysis.md:56-58` — Cross-reference step is vague ("scan all sections for tickers that appear in multiple agent outputs"). No formal scoring, no regime gating, no horizon weighting.

## 6. New-Agent Proposals

Four new agents prioritized by edge gap. Each comes with trigger conditions, model tier, MCP dependencies, output schema, and a system-prompt sketch.

### 6.1 — `gamma-flip-tracker` (priority 1, 0DTE)

**Purpose:** Identify today's pin (positive GEX) and trend (negative GEX) names, plus the strikes where dealer hedging flips.

**Trigger:** Every run of `/daily-analysis`. For ad-hoc, fires when a sweep agent surfaces a ticker — to check if the sweep is hitting dealer-short-gamma.

**Model tier:** Sonnet 4.6 (analytical, frequent).

**MCP dependencies:**
- `gamma_exposure_profile` (primary)
- `iv_term_structure` (regime confirm — backwardation amplifies negative GEX moves)
- `expiry_heatmap` (where today's volume sits)
- `greek_screener` (`min_gamma` filter for 0DTE plays)
- `most_active_contracts` (cross-ref strikes hit by flow)

**Output schema:**
```yaml
- ticker: SPY
  spot: 502.34
  zero_gamma_level: 504.10
  total_gex: -1.2B
  regime: NEGATIVE              # POSITIVE / NEGATIVE / FULLY_NEGATIVE / FULLY_POSITIVE
  read: "Below zero-gamma; dealer hedging is amplifying. Trend day risk."
  key_strikes:
    - {strike: 500, gex: -250M, role: "next short-gamma wall, breakdown trigger"}
    - {strike: 505, gex: +180M, role: "next long-gamma wall, magnet on bounce"}
  invalidation: "Spot reclaims 504.10 zero-gamma with > 0.3% range expansion"
  trade_bias: "Buy gamma / chase breakouts; do NOT sell premium today"
```

**System prompt sketch:**
> You map today's dealer gamma landscape. For SPY, QQQ, IWM and any ticker with > $500k 0DTE volume, call `gamma_exposure_profile` to get the zero-gamma level and per-strike GEX. Cross with `iv_term_structure` — backwardation + negative GEX = highest-conviction trend regime; contango + positive GEX = pin regime. Use `expiry_heatmap` to confirm volume is concentrated in today's expiry. Output for each ticker: spot, zero_gamma, total_gex, regime, the 2-3 most material gamma walls, a one-line read, an explicit invalidation (where the regime flips), and a trade bias (buy gamma vs sell premium). Skip tickers with INSUFFICIENT_DATA. Flag the SPX/SPY GEX state at the top — it sets the regime for everything else.

### 6.2 — `leap-positioning-radar` (priority 2, LEAP)

**Purpose:** Surface long-dated (DTE > 180) institutional positioning — the LEAP whales nobody currently tracks.

**Trigger:** Daily, but the bar is high — only flag tickers with multi-week persistence.

**Model tier:** Opus 4.7 (low-frequency, deep cross-referencing across many data sources).

**MCP dependencies:**
- `oi_trend` (multi-day OI build at LEAP expiries)
- `biggest_oi_increases` (filtered to LEAP DTE — needs the `min_dte` parameter from §5)
- `position_rolling_detector` (rolls forward = thesis durability)
- `largest_dark_pool_trades` + `dark_pool_price_levels` (institutional accumulation zones)
- `conviction_matrix` (DIRECTIONAL_LONG only — filter HEDGED_LONG/COVERED_CALL out)
- `stock_deep_dive` (fundamentals confirmation)
- `market_regime` (gating: only buy LEAPs in supportive regimes)

**Output schema:**
```yaml
- ticker: TSM
  scenario: DIRECTIONAL_LONG
  confidence_pct: 78
  leap_oi_growth_30d: +120k contracts at Jan-2027 calls
  dp_accumulation: "$280M last 30d, clustered at 175-180"
  rolls_detected: 2 in last 14 days, both forward
  fundamentals: "PE 22, sector tailwind (semis), analyst bullish 14/3/2"
  thesis: "Multi-quarter institutional build, defending 175 in DP, rolling near-term forward"
  invalidation: "Conviction matrix flips to HEDGED_LONG OR price breaks DP support at 175"
  suggested_structure: "Jan-2027 ITM call (delta 0.7) for capital efficiency vs holding equity"
```

**System prompt sketch:**
> You hunt LEAP-grade institutional setups (6-24mo horizon) — the slow accumulations that are too low-frequency for the daily flow agents to see. For each candidate ticker, demand: (a) `oi_trend` shows BUILDING with consecutive_build_days >= 5, (b) `biggest_oi_increases` filtered to DTE > 180 shows top-N positioning, (c) `largest_dark_pool_trades` and `dark_pool_price_levels` show accumulation clustered at consistent levels, (d) `conviction_matrix` returns DIRECTIONAL_LONG with confidence > 65 (reject HEDGED_LONG and COVERED_CALL — those are not LEAP buys), (e) `position_rolling_detector` shows rolls forward not unwinds, (f) `market_regime` is supportive (not bearish-trending). Use `stock_deep_dive` for fundamentals confirmation. Output only tickers passing 5+ of these gates. For each, propose a specific LEAP structure (ITM call, debit spread, etc.) and explicit invalidation.

### 6.3 — `vol-surface-scout` (priority 3, swing/event)

**Purpose:** Find skew/term-structure dislocations the rest of the system misses.

**Trigger:** Daily.

**Model tier:** Haiku 4.5 (high-frequency, structured analysis).

**MCP dependencies:**
- `iv_term_structure` (KINKED, BACKWARDATION names)
- `iv_rank_screener` (high IV = sell candidates, low IV = buy candidates)
- `iv_outliers` (single-contract IV blowups)
- `earnings_catalyst_scanner` (does the kink coincide with earnings?)
- **PROPOSED** `term_skew` (long-dated skew comparison)

**Output schema:**
```yaml
- ticker: NVDA
  structure: KINKED
  kink_expiry: 2026-05-30
  catalyst: "Earnings 2026-05-28 — kink is the event"
  iv_rank: 88
  bias: "SELL the kink — overpriced relative to historical earnings move"
  trade: "Iron condor 540/520 puts × 600/620 calls expiring 2026-05-30"
  invalidation: "Pre-earnings flow turns directional (analyst_vs_flow disagrees)"

- ticker: META
  structure: BACKWARDATION
  bias: "BUY back-month — front IV elevated, back cheap. Calendar play."
  trade: "Long Aug/short May ATM call calendar"
  invalidation: "Backwardation persists through next session — event-driven, not mispriced"
```

**System prompt sketch:**
> You scan the vol surface for dislocations. Run `iv_term_structure` across the watchlist and any ticker with elevated IV via `iv_rank_screener`. Flag every ticker returning KINKED — match the kink_expiry against `earnings_catalyst_scanner` to see if the kink IS the event. For BACKWARDATION names with no catalyst, flag as potential calendar-spread setups. For `iv_outliers`, identify single contracts with abnormal IV that could be mispricings vs whales hedging. Output: structure, kink_expiry, catalyst alignment, IV rank, bias (BUY vol / SELL vol / CALENDAR), and a specific trade structure. Reject signals where regime conflicts with the trade (don't sell premium in a regime where vol is expanding).

### 6.4 — `multileg-strategist` (priority 4, swing)

**Purpose:** Read the *structure* of institutional flow — spreads, calendars, condors, flies — to infer directional thesis with more nuance than raw call/put flow.

**Trigger:** Daily, focused on tickers showing high `multileg_ratio`.

**Model tier:** Sonnet 4.6.

**MCP dependencies:**
- `multileg_activity` (primary)
- `expiry_heatmap` (calendar vs vertical detection)
- `greek_screener` (delta-skew confirmation)
- `top_premium_trades` (single-leg vs multi-leg ratio)
- `gamma_exposure_profile` (where in the chain the spread sits)

**Output schema:**
```yaml
- ticker: AMD
  multileg_ratio: 0.42
  inferred_structure: "Bull call vertical, 130/140 May expiry"
  evidence: "Coordinated ask-side flow at 130C and bid-side at 140C, equal size"
  thesis: "Institution buying upside but capping risk — moderate conviction directional"
  invalidation: "Stock fails to break 125 within 2 weeks"
```

**System prompt sketch:**
> Spreads and combos imply specific directional theses with built-in risk caps — they're more informative than naked single-leg flow. Use `multileg_activity` to find tickers with multileg_ratio > 0.3. For each, cross with `expiry_heatmap` (same expiry across two strikes = vertical; different expiries = calendar) and `greek_screener` (delta skew across strikes confirms structure). Infer the most likely structure (vertical, calendar, fly, condor, ratio) and the directional thesis it implies. Output the inferred structure, evidence, thesis, and explicit invalidation. Skip tickers where the multileg activity looks like dealer hedging vs alpha (low premium / high volume / mid-quote prints).

## 7. Refactor Recommendations for `/daily-analysis`

### 7.1 — Restructure the report by horizon, not by agent

Current report (`daily-analysis.md:24-54`) sections: Regime / Sweeps / Accumulation / Fades / Earnings / Risk / Watchlist. This is *agent-centric*.

**Proposed:**
```
# Daily Market Analysis — YYYY-MM-DD

## Executive Summary
- Regime + GEX state (sets the entire day's bias)
- Top 0DTE play (or "no edge")
- Top swing build
- Top LEAP candidate
- Biggest risk

## 1. Regime & Gamma State              ← risk-monitor + gamma-flip-tracker

## 5. Volatility Surface                ← vol-surface-scout
## 6. Risk & Correlation                ← risk-monitor (using all candidates from 2-4 as input)
## 7. High-Conviction Cross-Ref         ← formal scoring rule, not vague text
```

### 7.2 — Add formal cross-reference scoring

Replace `daily-analysis.md:56-58` ("scan all sections for tickers that appear in multiple agent outputs") with explicit scoring:

```
Conviction score = Σ across agents of:
  +3  ticker flagged by gamma-flip-tracker as setting up a regime breakout
  +2  ticker has 3+ aligned signals in accumulation-hunter
  +2  ticker has multi-day OI build (oi_trend BUILDING, > 5 days)
  +2  conviction_matrix = DIRECTIONAL_LONG, confidence > 70
  +1  ticker shows in sweep-tracker top 5
  +1  ticker shows in earnings-scout BUY VOL or SELL VOL
  +1  ticker shows in multileg-strategist with directional structure
  -2  contrarian-scanner flags ticker as overcrowded long
  -1  risk-monitor flags ticker in correlation cluster
  -3  market_regime conflicts with the trade direction

Surface tickers with score >= 5 in the executive summary.
```

### 7.3 — Add `daily_synthesis` as a preflight step

`daily_synthesis` (insights.py) already returns regime + top bullish/bearish confluence + watchlist alerts in one call. Use it as Step 0 before spawning agents — it primes the regime context for every downstream agent and avoids redundant `market_regime` and `signal_confluence` calls.

### 7.4 — Make `risk-monitor` consume the other agents' output

Currently `risk-monitor` runs in parallel with the others (`daily-analysis.md:11-12`) so it correlates "the watchlist" rather than "today's high-conviction names." Better orchestration:

```
Phase 1 (parallel, ~1 min):
  - daily_synthesis preflight
  - gamma-flip-tracker
  - sweep-tracker
  - accumulation-hunter
  - earnings-scout
  - vol-surface-scout
  - multileg-strategist
  - contrarian-scanner
  - leap-positioning-radar

Phase 2 (sequential, depends on Phase 1):
  - risk-monitor receives the candidate set from Phase 1 and runs
    portfolio_correlation on TODAY'S candidates, not the static watchlist.
```

### 7.5 — Pipe winners into the watchlist for tomorrow

After scoring, call `manage_watchlist(action="add", group="conviction_YYYY-MM-DD", tickers=[...top_5...])`. This makes tomorrow's `risk-monitor` automatically track today's calls — closes the feedback loop the system currently lacks.

### 7.6 — Add `signal_backtest` to risk sizing

Each top-conviction ticker should have a sizing recommendation that scales with the historical win-rate of its triggering signal type. `risk-monitor` should call `signal_backtest` on the dominant signal class for each candidate and use the win_rate to weight position size in the executive summary.

### 7.7 — Multi-day metrics throughout

Every place an agent currently calls a single-day tool, audit whether the multi-day equivalent (`oi_trend`, `trend_analyzer`) is available — and use it. Single-day signals are noise; multi-day persistence is signal.
