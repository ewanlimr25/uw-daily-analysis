# Phase 4 — External Cross-Check (published evidence vs system methods)
**Date:** 2026-06-12. Nine method lanes verified by parallel researchers with live web verification (full citations, magnitudes, and counter-evidence in `p4_external_evidence.json`; the sweep lane includes full-text extraction of Pan & Poteshman 2006). Verdicts are for **the system's specific usage**, not the general topic.

| Method (system usage) | External verdict | What the literature actually says vs what the system does |
|---|---|---|
| **Dark-pool accumulation** (+3 line: DP blocks + OI = informed accumulation, days–weeks drift) | **CONTRADICTED** | Zhu (2014 RFS): informed traders *avoid* dark pools (selection toward uninformed). Comerton-Forde & Putniņš (2015 JFE): dark **blocks** are the *least* informed trade category. Hatheway et al. (2017 JFQA): average dark execution is the less-informed side. Only published informativeness is **15–120 minutes** in small algo slices (Nimalendran & Ray 2014), not blocks, not weeks. Dasgupta-Prat-Verardo (2011 JF): persistent institutional accumulation predicts **reversal** at the tail. The internal core reproduces numerically (P2), but its external premise is inverted — the +3 weight rests on practitioner narrative |
| **Dealer DEX/vanna swing signal** (+3 line, 1–4wk direction) | **UNSUPPORTED at this usage** (folklore) | **No peer-reviewed support** exists for DEX-flip or vanna-squeeze as a 1–4-week directional signal (SqueezeMetrics/Karsan are unreviewed). Baltussen et al. (2021 JFE): hedging-induced pressure is *intraday and mean-reverting*. What IS supported: GEX-regime → next-day vol (Ni-Pearson-Poteshman-White 2021 RFS; Barbon-Buraschi) — the §2/§2a *advisory* lane, with the caveat that one 8-yr SPY replication finds GEX→vol subsumed by VIX controls. Internal live misfire (P2) + external void: the heaviest rubric line has the weakest evidence base in the system |
| **GEX walls as magnets** (already advisory, internally refuted) | **CONTRADICTED (concordant)** | NPP (2005 JFE) pinning is OPEX-day clustering only; Golez-Jackwerth (2012): pinning can invert; a 2026 study (Elms) documents a modern regime shift from pinning to **amplification** — externally corroborating the internal anti-magnet result (26.7% vs 50%). The system's NO_GO handling is exactly right; walls are better used as range-expansion flags |
| **Single-leg put whale (C19, advisory)** | **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED** (strongest scored-candidate) | Bearish-option informativeness is real: Xing-Zhang-Zhao (2010), Cremers-Weinbaum (2010); the correct asymmetry citation is **Johnson & So (2012 JFE)** (short-sale-cost channel), *not* Pan-Poteshman (P-P is two-sided). Ge-Lin-Pearson (2016 JFE) found opening **call** buys most informative on signed ISE data — so "calls are beta" is regime-conditional, not structural. Next-session horizon matches the published 1–2-day public-signal decay. Plan implication: condition the put lane on short-sale-constraint proxies (borrow/DTC — the fz lane exists); don't hard-code the call NO_GO cross-regime |
| **Sweep momentum** (narrative-only + C4 opening gate) | **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED** | P-P 2006 verified at full text: open-buy volume ~40bps/day, ~1%/wk, no reversal — but the **publicly-inferred** version predicts 1–2 days then *reverses* and loses significance once open-buy is controlled. Ge-Lin-Pearson independently confirm opening-volume primacy (validates C4). Muravyev (2016 JF): much post-print drift is dealer *inventory* pressure that mean-reverts. Bryzgalova et al. (2023 JF): post-2020 aggressive option flow is heavily retail (the ≥$500K floor is the right direction). Equity ISO literature (Chakravarty et al. 2012) does not transfer to options sweeps — no peer-reviewed options-sweep study exists. The system's 0-scoring of sweeps is externally correct |
| **PC-ratio contrarian fade** (−2 line) | **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED** (mechanism mis-attributed) | Single-name P/C extremes predict **continuation**, not reversal (P-P; Blau et al. 2014; GLP 2016) — fading a call-heavy single name fights informed flow. Index-level contrarian P/C works only at *fear* extremes, 10–30d (Simon-Wiggins 2001). The internal −22pp standalone backtest agrees with the literature. The −2 line survives only re-framed as an informed-flow-continuation penalty; the fade-able crowd is retail option *demand*, best harvested as vol-selling (de Silva-Smith-So 2026), which is what §2a does |
| **IV term-structure earnings** (+1 BUY/SELL VOL) | **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED** (timing > direction) | DJKS (2019 RFS 32:646) validates the kink as the right measurement object — and implies kink **presence is mechanical** before every event (zero discriminating power by itself — concordant with the live 58/61-BACKWARDATION degeneracy). Alexiou et al. (2025 RoF): front-end *concavity*, not backwardation, discriminates rich events. Gao-Xing-Zhang (2018 JFQA): buy-early (T−3, exit pre-event) earns +3.34% gross but decays post-publication and is cost-fragile. Seller edge ≈ +80bps vol, regime-unstable (reversed 2011–15; 2025–26 practitioner data shows buyers winning). Entry-window discipline matters more than the BUY/SELL verdict |
| **Sector rotation persistence** (+1 conditional) | **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED** | Industry momentum exists (Moskowitz-Grinblatt 1999) but is *returns*-formed, long-side, large-cap; flow-persistence continuation (Coval-Stafford 2007, Lou 2012) is **uninformed pressure that fully reverses**; 2024 evidence finds generalized sector rotation indistinguishable from chance net of costs. Options-flow predictability horizon is days-to-2-weeks, shorter than the agent's multi-week mandate. +1-conditional/advisory is the correct ceiling |
| **LEAP long-dated positioning** (+2 rolls, LEAP book) | **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED → mostly contradicted as "informed"** | Informed traders concentrate **short-dated OTM** (Augustin et al. 2019 M&A evidence); the one pro-LEAP paper was rewritten by its own authors into a securities-lending/financing story; Muravyev-Pearson-Pollet (2025 JFE): ≥2/3 of option-implied predictability is **borrow fees**, not conviction. Lakonishok et al. (2007): standing long-dated OI is overwriting/income — which *validates* the agent's HEDGED_LONG/COVERED_CALL rejections. Conditional support only when catalyst timing is uncertain (Augustin et al. 2023). Keep advisory/low-weight; add a financing/borrow-fee disqualifier |
| **0DTE premium selling** (§2a advisory) | **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED** (basis + costs unresolved) | VRP family robust (Carr-Wu 2009; BTZ 2009 — 30d horizon). 0DTE-specific: Vilkov (2024/26): win rates match (90–95%) but unconditional open-entry short premium is ~zero gross, negative net of costs; conditioning (VIX-state, structure) is where net-positive results live — concordant with the system's conditioning stack. Muravyev-Ni (2020 JFE) + Papagelis-Dotsis (2025): for *fixed-maturity* options the premium is earned **overnight**, not intraday — challenges the never-overnight framing's generality (0DTE-specific structure differs; an open-sold 0DTE has no overnight). Tail: published ES(1%) makes win-rate the wrong promotion metric — expectancy-with-tail-prior only. Internal +0.30–0.39%/day needs an explicit PnL basis and net-of-cost restatement before being quoted |

## Cross-cutting conclusions for P6
1. **The external evidence ranks the system's lanes almost exactly inversely to its rubric weights.** Heaviest scored lines (+3 DP accumulation, +3 DEX) have contradicted/absent external bases; the validated-edge families (bearish option informativeness, VRP harvesting, opening-flow conditioning, GEX→vol) live in 0-point advisory lanes or sizing gates.
2. **Three system design choices the literature endorses:** the C4 opening-flow gate (P-P + GLP), the ≥$500K institutional floor (Bryzgalova retail pollution), and the LEAP agent's overwriting rejections (Lakonishok). These should be called out as keep-and-protect.
3. **Horizon discipline is the recurring external correction:** public option-flow signals decay in 1–2 days (often reversing); flow-pressure continuation reverses at weeks; the system's next-session lanes match the evidence, its multi-week directional lanes don't.
4. **Citation fixes:** bearish asymmetry → Johnson & So (2012); earnings term structure → DJKS RFS 2019; "spreads more informative than single-leg" (multileg agent) has no support — Chakravarty et al. 2004 finds price discovery in short-dated OTM, agnostic on leg count.
