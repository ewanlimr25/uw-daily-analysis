## 2. What prior audits missed (new in this audit)

1. **`signal-confluence` is a composite re-count, leveraged 4×** (P1 F1). Prior audits treated it as an ordinary tool — promoted +19.5pp (05-23), retracted from the LB gate (05-30 D3) — but never ran the factor-list probe showing it server-side re-aggregates `dp_accumulation`/`oi_building`/`bullish_flow`, i.e. the same quantities the rubric already scores. The +2 line and the entry-gate role survived every prior audit untouched; the structural identification is new (and explains the method-instability they observed: the tool's MC is whatever its constituent factors' MC happens to be that window).
2. **`signal-backtest` defect quantification** (P1 F2 / P3 §2). 05-23 found it returning empty; 05-25 called it regime-confounded; nobody measured the forward-window clamp (+2.5pp recency inflation, clamped rows 0.733 vs complete 0.485) or the 30pp `--top-n` instability, and nobody flagged that "per ticker per class" is unimplementable (no `--symbol` flag) while envelopes stamp one market-wide stat per ticker.
3. **Dead-letter rubric lines** (P1 F5, P2 battery): −3 regime / −1 cluster never fire as points (0/151) — published rubric overstates its own discipline. Unnoticed across six audits because the embedded rubric was always read as documentation, not tested against `score_components`.
4. **Debate gate has never recorded a verdict** (0/151). 06-06 P2.1 asked for the residual pair to be stored; the harder fact — the `gate_verdicts.debate` key is absent on every call ever — was not surfaced.
5. **Live trigger-semantics failures on the heaviest scored lines**: +3 DEX awarded with no sign flip (MU/MRVL/ASML, 06-11); earnings PRIMARY kink gate structurally unreachable; FDX full-size on an 11-contract tenor; opex `probability` field doesn't exist; sector persistence gate passed 11/11; Goyal-Saretto percentile running on 43/252 days. Prior audits graded *outcomes*; none executed the agents' own tool protocols against their definitions. (Important reconciliation: 05-29 crowned `dealer_positioning` "the only honest class" at 80% realized — consistent with my finding once decomposed: positive DEX *level* in an up-tape is a working beta-long, which is why 05-30 also measured it −7pp *excess*. 6. **Spec-drift inventory**: weekly entry gate ≥5 vs rubric ≥4 (undecidable award); thin-window rule *lowering* the HIGH bar; daily/weekly sizing-floor wording divergence; agent-vs-rubric threshold conflict (LEAP >65 vs >70); no `rubric_version` in the envelope (06-06 noted era-stratification pain but didn't propose version pinning).

## 3. Where this audit disagrees with prior audit decisions

1. **The re-weighting cadence itself.** Even after 05-30's own "DEFER all component re-weights" (correct), 06-06 still shipped a weight change (P1.4 demote) and the ≥9 HIGH cut (05-30 P1.3) went live flagged in-sample-only — and failed its scheduled re-confirmation today (P3 §3). Disagreement: weight/threshold changes should require pre-registered, cross-regime, BH-surviving evidence — i.e., **freeze the rubric** except for structural de-duplication; the audit cycle should grade, not retune.
2. **Treating `win_rate` as a salvageable per-call number.** Prior audits layered caps, floors, fallbacks, and excess gates on top of `signal-backtest`. Given P3 §2 (parameter noise exceeds any claimed edge) and the anti-calibration result, the honest object is a *class-level, complete-window, pinned-parameter* statistic with explicit n — or no number at all. The cap machinery treats the symptom.
3. **05-29 P0.2's gate-swap instinct** (already retracted by 05-30) and any future tool-tier reshuffling: with the F1 composite identification, LB-gate membership debates are moot until the gate requires *quantity* independence (premium prints vs ΔOI vs DP volume vs dealer-greek response), not tool-name diversity.
4. **fz/C-register lanes**: no disagreement — the advisory quarantine discipline (C15–C19, C28, C31) is working as designed and C19 now has its first second-regime accrual (P3: June 0.632).

## 4. Net position after reconciliation
The prior audits correctly diagnosed *that* the scored layer wasn't ranking outcomes; this audit adds *why* (correlated additive lines re-counting one footprint through a composite; a sizing substrate whose noise dominates its signal; trigger semantics nobody enforced) and verifies *where the real edges live* (Tier-1 puts, 0DTE VRP, the brake stack — all currently 0-point lanes). That inversion — scored lanes unvalidated, advisory lanes validated — is the central fact the P6 plan must act on.
